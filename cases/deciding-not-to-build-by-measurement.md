# Deciding not to build, by measurement

## Question
Can the projections price only the starting quarterback?

## Measured
Best pregame starter call 91.5%; two sources agreeing 95.5% on 88% of games; break-even ~97%.

## Control
Three allocators met every budget yet each lost the held-out replay (4 cells worse); refused.

## Changed
The rule was closed unbuilt; the honest product prints a per-team verdict (14 / 12 / 11 of 32 teams over). That verdict was measured before one quarterback per world and full-game lines; withdrawn until N2c reruns it.
