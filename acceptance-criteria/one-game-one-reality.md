# One game, one reality (the project owner's law, written 2026-11-09 before its check's first run)

Sentences served: "His team gets about S of them, because ___" (the team level) and "Of the A throws, the receivers get Y ... because ___" (the group level).

1. A simulated game is one reality. In it, each team has one starting quarterback, and everything below him in the chain (the team's attempts and carries, the play calls, how targets spread) is computed in that reality.
2. The quarterback is a causal root of who throws; what else a backup start moves is measured, not assumed. Measured 2026-11-09 on the play-by-play record's stats file, within team-season, regular season before each final week, a clean backup start being a game where the team-season's main quarterback threw no pass and a clean main start one where no other quarterback threw more than 3 (423 clean backup starts, 109 team-seasons): team pass attempts -1.8 a game (2SE 1.6) and targets -1.6 (2SE 1.6), ESTABLISHED but barely, and not two confirmations (targets are nearly the attempts); carries -0.6 (2SE 1.3) and the top target-getter's share -0.7 points (2SE 1.5), NOT SHOWN. Corrected the same day: the first measurement (attempts -6.8, carries -5.7, targets -6.4, top share -2.7 points) counted mid-game changes and final-week games as backup starts; those were most of it. Until measured otherwise, a backup world changes who throws and about 1.8 attempts; carries and how targets spread are carried over unchanged.
3. Two lines that cannot both happen in one game carry different world labels and are never added up as one game. At most one quarterback can throw more than half of his team's passes in a game, so two quarterback lines on one team that each claim more than half of the team's attempts are two worlds. Checks that add lines up (the team budgets) add them within a world; a player's expected line is the chance of each world times his line in it.
4. Judged by `checks/check_one_game_one_reality.py` on the card: NOT MET while any team carries two full-game quarterback lines in one world. A card with no world label is one world, so the card read NOT MET on 2024 week 15 (7 teams: CAR, CLE, IND, MIA, NO, PIT, TEN). From 2026-11-10 the card draws one quarterback per world (slate-kit/draws.py quarterback_worlds, weighted by P(starts)) and names each quarterback line's world. Open: the backup world's lower attempts (clause 2, about 1.8, barely established) is not yet applied, because it would change the engine's team totals the demo's provenance protects.
