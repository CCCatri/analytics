# R4 TARGETS - downs (rules sentence, clause 3: "four downs to gain ten"), set before any code. A record read, not product.
Frozen on the same three grounds as clock-check.md: an injected case fails, a rule article contradicts a line, or a measured property of the play-by-play record; each edit prints its reason.
No cutoff, pass rate or tolerance is set in advance.

## Instruments (through record.events; names read from the header before use)
down, ydstogo, yardline_100, posteam, penalty, kind (play_type), two_point_attempt; plays are ordered by order_sequence (measured 2026-10-26: the quarter clock runs up once in six seasons under it).
Series = consecutive scrimmage snaps by one team from a first down to the next first down, a score, or a change of possession.

## Counting
Per season, per check: AS RULE / EXPLAINED (each by the rule the play-by-play record names, with its article) / OTHER (each listed with its rows, never filled) / UNCHECKED (a check empty, counted by kind). Postseason games printed separately.

## Checks
C1 (3-8-2, 3-8-4) Downs run 1 to 4: no snap carries a down above 4; the snap after a charged down carries the next down unless a new series began; the snap after a down cancelled by a foul repeats that down, unless the foul gave a first down (14-1-2 Item 5) or a loss of down (14-1-2 Item 2).
C2 (3-8-3) The line to gain: a first down carries ydstogo 10, or the distance to the goal line when that is nearer (and goal). Any other first-down distance is EXPLAINED only by a foul enforced before that snap (for example first-and-25, 14-4-9 Item 1; a pre-snap foul turning 1st and 10 into 1st and 15), listed with the foul.
C3 (3-8-2, 7-3-2a) Four downs: a fourth down that ends short of the line to gain, with no foul giving a new series, is followed by the other team's snap (turnover on downs) or by a kick, or by no snap because the half or the game ran out (4-1; 16-1 in overtime), the last counted EXPLAINED with its period; any other follow-up is listed.
C4 (7-3-1d, 7-3-1e) A kicking-team recovery starts a new series for the kickers: their next snap is a first down.
C5 (3-40, 11-3-1) Tries sit outside any series: no Try carries a down, and the next team's first snap after the kickoff is a first down.
