# Possession ends: one definition, every use (the project owner's law, written 2026-11-09 before its quality gate's first run)

Rule sentence served: "the ball changes hands after every score or stop" (the rules level). A possession ends in two parts, and both are carried everywhere.

1. One definition. How a possession ends is (the stop, the score on the play), read by `product/rules.py` (`possession_stop`, `score_on_play`, `possession_ends`) and by nothing else. The stop is one of eight, read from the offense's last snap: punt (3-35-3(c)), field-goal try (3-35-3(e), 11-4), interception (3-35-3(a)), lost fumble (3-35-3(b)), touchdown (11-2-1), safety (11-5-1), turnover on downs (3-35-3(d), 7-3-2; whatever the clock), clock (4-1-1, 4-8-1). The score on the play is the first change on the scoreboard from that snap to the next snap or kickoff, Tries left out: offense 6, 3 or 2; other side 6 or 2; none (11-1-2). Terms as in `VOCABULARY.md`.
2. Every use, complete. Any use of the engine's games (a check, a forecast, a card, a handoff) reads possession ends through `rules.possession_ends` and carries all of it: every (stop, score) cell, record and engine side by side, per game; a cell that is zero on one side prints as 0 and is never dropped. A use that reads a part (scores without the stop, the stop without the score, the offense's points without the other side's) is not accepted.
3. Outside judge. On the play-by-play record, every possession's (stop, score) names nflfastR's `fixed_drive_result` under `rules.nflfastr_result`, except the named cases in `checks/check_possessions_crosswalk.py`, each with its play and reason.
4. Engine against record (a quality gate, judged by `checks/check_possession_ends.py`, the next accepted change): each cell's engine rate per game within the record's at 2SE; a cell the record holds above zero at 2SE that the engine never plays is NOT MET. Until every cell is met, the engine's scores are not accepted as forecasts; the quality gate lists the cells not met.

Measured 2026-11-09 at bba2ad4 (record: 36,349 possessions, 1,676 games; engine: 2024 week 15, 30 draws, 480 games): every record possession has one stop and one score; nflfastR agrees on 36,344, 4 sit in its 2 pinned numbering errors, 4 are named. The other side scores 1.599 points a game in the record and 0.192 in the engine (safeties only: the engine plays no pick-six, fumble return, punt or kick return, or blocked kick); the offense scores 39.48 a game in the record and 35.89 in the engine.
