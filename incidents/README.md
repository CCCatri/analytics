# incidents/ — the evidence behind the regression case

`predecessor-index-mismatch/` holds two snapshots of the predecessor program: `revision-12-fix-without-regression-test/` (the fix that passed its checks without a test that could have caught the bug) and `revision-16-with-regression-test/` (the same fix with `tests/synth.py` and `tests/test_product.py`, which fail on the defect). `INCIDENT.md` is the record; `incident-derived-rules.md` the rules drawn from it. `run.sh` stage 3 runs both snapshots and `checks/test_incident_baseline.py` requires revision 12 to report its two known failures exactly and revision 16 none. The story is told in `cases/regression-test-needed-data-that-could-trigger-the-bug.md`.
